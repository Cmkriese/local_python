# Manning Flow

## Purpose
Provide a web-based Manning open-channel flow calculator and comparison tool. Detailed requirements will be added as the app is designed.

## Inputs
Every value the user can enter or change will be defined here with units, ranges, defaults, and controls.

## Calculations
Manning equation relationships and all variables will be documented here before implementation.

## Outputs
The app will report calculated flow, velocity, depth, area, hydraulic radius, and other results in clearly labeled units.

## Look and feel
The app will use a clean, responsive, engineering-focused interface.

## Validation
Hand-calculation test cases and accepted output tolerances will be added here.

## Out of scope
Temporary and advanced hydraulic behaviors will be identified here before implementation.
